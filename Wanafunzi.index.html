About Us

This website is developed by THE KBA SCHOOL KAHAWA WEST LEARNERS, for their Mombasa Mini Games competition.

Wanafunzi is dedicated to guiding learners in discovering their career paths early. Our mission is to align talents, interests, and academic performance with Competency-Based Education (CBE) pathways.

Vision: Empower every learner to achieve their dreams.

Values: Integrity, Innovation, Inclusivity.

Who We Serve

We serve primary and secondary learners across Kenya, helping them explore career opportunities based on their strengths and interests. Teachers and parents also benefit from insights to support learners’ growth.
